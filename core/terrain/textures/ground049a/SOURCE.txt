Used as the default near-field ground detail (albedo + NormalGL + roughness)
blended into the terrain drape within a few metres of the camera by
core/terrain/terrain_detail.gdshader (see TerrainBuilder.apply_near_detail_textures).
Only Color, NormalGL, and Roughness are bundled; Displacement/AO/blend/usdc are not needed.
